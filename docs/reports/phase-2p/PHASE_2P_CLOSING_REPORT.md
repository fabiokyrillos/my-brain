## 1. What the phase was for, and whether it did it

The owner opened Phase 2P by naming twelve concrete everyday failures rather
than a theme: text-only capture on Today, modality tabs on Capture, a
confirmation queue that did not clear, excessive confirmation, broken and hidden
Conversation, nested person-company editing, thin memory creation, list-first
Relations, visually weak Calendar and Reminders, notification preferences mixed
with history, and a single scrolling Settings page.

**All twelve are addressed.** The queue clears atomically because the entry
lifecycle is re-derived rather than overridden; Conversation was fixed at a root
cause that turned out not to be the provider path at all; capture is one
composer with text ready on arrival; Settings is eight documents; Relations
opens on a drawing with a complete text alternative; the calendar has a real
month; reminders are created in a dialog; and Notificações is a history surface
with its preferences elsewhere.

## 4. The four things this phase got right that were not obvious

### A slice that builds everything and enables nothing

Slice 2P.4 delivered the whole automation contract — per-category policy,
calibration, evidence, audit, undo, a visible control — and **enabled no
category**, because the reference set does not exist and the producer is an
`after insert` trigger that cannot see rows that already existed. Backfilling
would have manufactured evidence for reviews that predate the contract. The
slice ended at an owner checkpoint, which was the correct outcome rather than a
failure to finish.

Four of the six categories have **no producer at all**, because the product has
no review flow for projects, companies, memories or relations. That is different
in kind from "not enough evidence yet": waiting accumulates nothing. It is
recorded as four named remainders rather than as a number that will grow.

### A requirement that named a column that does not exist

`2P-REMINDER-002` asked the reminder dialog to group recurrence. `reminders` has
no recurrence column of any kind. The owner corrected the requirement rather
than the schema, and the surface has **no control, no disabled control, no
"coming soon", and no placeholder inviting a rule to be typed into a title.**

### A requirement that named a construct the product deliberately does not have

`2P-ACCESS-003` asked for ARIA tabs. Five surfaces navigate between separate
documents by link, each with a signed decision saying so. The owner interpreted
the requirement as the navigation the product actually uses — links,
`aria-current="page"`, visible focus, keyboard, back and forward — rather than
reversing five decisions to satisfy a word. All five are now proved on three
browser lanes.

### A recorded fact that was false, and only a mutation control could say so

Slice 2P.7 recorded that *"no action on the reminders page has ever actually
re-rendered it"*, and the PRD repeated it. Slice 2P.8 was authorized to repair
the consequence and, reproducing first, found the claim **false**: the list does
re-render in place. It was settled by deleting the revalidation, rebuilding,
restarting the server and re-running — the assertion failed, and restoring the
line made it pass.

`revalidatePath` accepts a literal resolved segment; what it cannot reach is a
route that is not the URL it names. `/pt-BR/app/reminders` *is* the rendered
URL, so it works; `/pt-BR/app/settings` was not, because that surface lives
under a second dynamic segment. **The generalisation was wrong, not the
measurement it came from.**

**No repair was written**, because the defect does not reproduce — 12 of 12
consecutive creations, desktop and phone. What was written instead is proof that
the properties hold, so a regression cannot return in silence.

## 7. What the next phase should know

1. **The evidence hierarchy earned its place.** Three defects this phase found
   were invisible below a real browser, and two more were invisible outside
   WebKit. A lane that does not render the thing does not prove the thing.
2. **A probe that fails is not a product that fails.** Six probe defects were
   found and each was verified in the sources before anything moved — a partial
   accessible-name computation accused working code, and a framework's own route
   announcer read as an interruption. **A guard that fails on correct code is
   the one that gets weakened later.**
3. **A stop condition is cheap and a wrong migration is not.** Three needs were
   raised to the owner with evidence instead of being written. Two were funded
   and one was refused, and the refused one is a remainder rather than a gap.
4. **Records are preserved, arithmetic is corrected forward.** Two corrections
   landed this phase — a count and a false measurement — and neither rewrote a
   signature.
